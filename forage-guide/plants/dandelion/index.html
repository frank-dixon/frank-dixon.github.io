Forb Spring Fall

Taraxacum officinale

A common New England green for rabbits and poultry, and a normal part of pasture.

Who can eat it

  • Rabbit Safe

    Dandelion is a mild, widely used green for rabbits and poultry and is grazed by ruminants.

    Keep lawn chemicals out of the basket. A handful is a supplement, not a meal.

Identification

A basal rosette of toothed leaves, hollow flower stalks, and a single yellow head. The plant has milky sap.

Lookalikes

Catsear has branched, solid stalks and hairy leaves. Hawkweeds are hairy. Those are not substitutes without their own identification.

Parts to feed

Leaves and flowers. Roots are not needed for feed.

New England timing

The best leaves are April through early June, then again in September and October. Midsummer leaves are bitter. Flowers follow the spring leaves.

Harvest

Cut leaves from unsprayed ground. Wash grit off. Skip plants from a chemically treated lawn.

Preparation

Feed fresh or dry the leaves. No wilting is required for safety. Large wet amounts can loosen stools.
