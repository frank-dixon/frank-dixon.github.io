Forb Toxic to some animals Summer Fall

Datura stramonium

A tropane-alkaloid plant. Seeds and leaves are both dangerous.

Who can eat it

  • Rabbit Toxic

    Atropine, scopolamine, and hyoscyamine. Small amounts of leaf or seed cause severe poisoning.

    Do not feed it.

Identification

Large toothed leaves, a foul smell, white or pale purple trumpet flowers, and a spiny egg-shaped pod.

Lookalikes

Ground cherry has a papery husk and is not a reason to experiment. If the plant has a spiny pod and a trumpet flower, do not feed it.

Parts to feed

Do not feed leaves, flowers, or seeds.

New England timing

It appears in New England barnyards and gardens in midsummer and carries spiny pods into frost.

Harvest

Pull it away from pens. Do not put it in the feed.

Preparation

No preparation makes it feed.
