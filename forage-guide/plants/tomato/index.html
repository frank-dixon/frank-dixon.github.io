Garden Toxic to some animals Summer Fall

Solanum lycopersicum

The vine is a nightshade. Leaves and green fruit are not feed.

Identification

A hairy, strong-smelling vine you planted, with yellow flowers and green fruit that ripens red, yellow, or another ripe color.

Lookalikes

Horsenettle is spiny and wild. Potato vines are a separate toxic entry. Do not feed any of them.

Parts to feed

Do not feed leaves, stems, or green fruit.

New England timing

Vines are up from June until frost. Green fruit and leaves are the problem all season. Ripe fruit is late summer.

Harvest

Do not toss suckers and prunings into a pen.

Preparation

Ripe red fruit, seeds and all, is sometimes a small treat for chickens and pigs. It is not forage, and it is not a rabbit food in this guide.
