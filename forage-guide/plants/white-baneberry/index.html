Forb Toxic to some animals Spring Summer

Actaea pachypoda · doll's eyes, red baneberry

A woodland plant with poisonous berries and roots.

Identification

Compound leaves, a small cluster of white flowers, then white berries on thick red stalks, each berry with a black dot. Red baneberry has red berries.

Lookalikes

False Solomon's seal has a different flower cluster and is not a feed in this guide. Do not feed woodland berries to animals.

Parts to feed

Do not feed berries, roots, or leaves.

New England timing

It flowers in late spring in rich New England woods. The white berries with black dots, doll's eyes, are obvious later in summer. Red baneberry (Actaea rubra) gets the same verdict.

Harvest

Do not gather it.

Preparation

No preparation makes it feed.
