Vaccinium corymbosum

A native shrub. The fruit is the part animals actually get. Leaves are not a bulk feed.

Who can eat it

  • Rabbit Limit

    Ripe blueberries are an acceptable treat. The shrub is not a leaf forage, and it grows next to laurels that are toxic.

    Be sure the bush is blueberry before you pick. Do not feed leaves as the product.

Identification

An upright shrub, alternate oval leaves, white urn-shaped flowers, and blue berries with a bloom.

Lookalikes

Sheep laurel has pink saucer flowers and opposite or whorled leaves and is deadly. If the shrub is evergreen with pink saucers, it is not blueberry.

Parts to feed

Ripe berries as a treat. Leaves only in a small accidental amount.

New England timing

Fruit ripens in July and August in most of New England, earlier on warm coastal sites, later in the mountains and in Maine.

Harvest

Pick berries. Do not cut the bushes for leaf fodder.

Preparation

Feed fruit fresh, in modest amounts. It is sugar, not a forage base.
