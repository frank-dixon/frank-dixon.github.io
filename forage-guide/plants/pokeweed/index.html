Forb Toxic to some animals Summer Fall

Phytolacca americana

A tall native plant of New England edges and gardens. It is poisonous. The berries stain and tempt people who have heard they are food.

Who can eat it

  • Rabbit Toxic

    Phytolaccatoxin and related compounds. The root is the most dangerous part. Leaves and berries poison livestock too.

    Do not feed it. Do not treat the purple berries as a poultry treat.

Identification

Large alternate leaves, a red-purple stem, and drooping clusters of dark purple berries. The plant can be taller than a person.

Lookalikes

Elderberry is a woody shrub with opposite leaves and red-purple stems on a bush, not this herb. Neither is a safe leafy feed.

Parts to feed

Do not feed shoots, leaves, berries, or roots.

New England timing

Shoots come up in May. Berries ripen purple in late summer and fall. The root is the most toxic part and is present all season.

Harvest

Do not cut it for a basket.

Preparation

No livestock preparation makes pokeweed feed.
