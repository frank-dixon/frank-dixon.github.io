Rubus idaeus

Garden and wild red raspberry. Leaves are used like blackberry leaves.

Who can eat it

  • Rabbit Safe

    Raspberry leaves are a standard minor green for rabbits and are browsed by goats.

    Keep the portion modest. Thorns are a handling problem, not a toxin.

Identification

Upright canes with a whitish bloom, leaves usually in threes, and a red fruit that slips off the core.

Lookalikes

Blackberry fruit keeps its core. Wineberry, an invasive, has red hairs on the cane. Wineberry leaves are not a separate entry; do not assume every cane is raspberry.

Parts to feed

Leaves and tender tips. Ripe fruit as a small treat for poultry and pigs.

New England timing

Leaves from May through August. Fruit in July in southern New England, later north.

Harvest

Cut leafy tips, not the whole cane.

Preparation

Fresh or dried leaves. No special wilting.
