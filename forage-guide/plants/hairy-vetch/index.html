Legume Toxic to some animals Spring Summer

Vicia villosa

A climbing vetch that has poisoned cattle. It is not a cut-and-carry feed.

Who can eat it

  • Rabbit Toxic Uncertain

    Hairy vetch has caused fever, skin disease, and death in cattle. It is not safe cut-and-carry forage for the other animals in this guide either.

    Remove it from mixed cuttings. Seeds are part of the problem.

Identification

Hairy stems, purple pea flowers in one-sided clusters, and many small leaflets.

Lookalikes

Garden pea vines are hairless, with larger leaflets and tendrils on a plant you planted. Bird vetch is similar enough that a weedy vetch should not be fed.

Parts to feed

Do not feed leaves, seeds, or hay made from it.

New England timing

A cover crop and roadside vine. It flowers in early summer. Poisoning cases are associated with grazing and with hay.

Harvest

Pull it out of a mixed bundle.

Preparation

Drying does not make hairy vetch safe.
