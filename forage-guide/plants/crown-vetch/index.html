Legume Toxic to some animals Summer

Securigera varia

A pink-flowered roadside legume that is poisonous, not a forage vetch.

Who can eat it

  • Rabbit Toxic

    Crown vetch contains nitroglycosides. It has poisoned livestock and is not a forage.

    Do not include it because goats or cattle sometimes pick at it on a bank.

Identification

Sprawling stems, compound leaves, and a circle of pink-and-white pea flowers. Seed pods are slender.

Lookalikes

Birdsfoot trefoil has yellow flowers. Hairy vetch has purple flowers and obviously hairy stems.

Parts to feed

Do not feed any part.

New England timing

It flowers along New England roads from June into August. It was planted for erosion control and escaped.

Harvest

Keep it out of the basket.

Preparation

There is no preparation that makes it a feed.
