Garden Spring Summer

Pisum sativum

The vines and pods of the garden pea. Sweet pea (Lathyrus odoratus) is a different, poisonous flower and is not this plant.

Identification

A hairless vine you planted, with tendrils, white flowers, and pea pods.

Lookalikes

Sweet pea is an ornamental with different flowers and poisonous seeds. Hairy vetch is hairy and purple-flowered and is toxic. If you did not plant it, do not treat it as a garden pea.

Parts to feed

Vines, leaves, and pods of garden pea.

New England timing

Vines are available as the pods fill, often June and July, and again in a fall planting in warmer parts of the region.

Harvest

Cut spent vines from the garden row.

Preparation

Feed fresh. Do not feed moldy vines.
