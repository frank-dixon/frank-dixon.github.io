Forb Summer

Amaranthus retroflexus

A coarse garden and barnyard weed. Nitrate is the hazard.

Who can eat it

  • Rabbit Limit

    Pigweed is a nitrate accumulator. Cattle have been poisoned by large amounts of stressed plants. Smaller species should not get a heavy feeding either.

    A few young leaves in a mix are a different risk from a trough of pigweed. When in doubt, leave the stand.

Identification

A red taproot, oval leaves, and a bristly green flower cluster. The stem is rough.

Lookalikes

Lambsquarters has a white mealy tip. Spiny amaranth has sharp spines and should be left alone.

Parts to feed

Young leaves, if at all, and never a cartload from rich ground.

New England timing

Midsummer into early fall. Nitrate is highest in stressed or heavily fertilized plants, which are common in gardens and barnyards.

Harvest

Skip plants that were drought-stressed and then heavily manured.

Preparation

There is no home preparation that removes nitrate.
