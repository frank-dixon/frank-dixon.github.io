Kalmia angustifolia · lambkill

A low shrub, very common in the region, and seriously poisonous.

Identification

A low evergreen shrub, opposite or whorled narrow leaves, and clusters of pink flowers along the stem, not only at the tip.

Lookalikes

Mountain laurel is taller, with flowers in a terminal cluster. Both are toxic. Blueberry has alternate leaves and a blueberry flower, not a Kalmia saucer.

Parts to feed

Do not feed leaves or flowers.

New England timing

It blooms pink in June on New England barrens, pastures, and bog edges. The leaves are toxic all season. The old name lambkill is literal.

Harvest

Do not browse a pasture shrub that looks like this.

Preparation

No preparation makes it feed.
