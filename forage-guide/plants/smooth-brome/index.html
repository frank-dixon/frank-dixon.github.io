Bromus inermis

A sod-forming hay grass used in New England mixes.

Identification

A hairless grass with a drooping, open flower head and a small M-shaped watermark on some young leaves.

Lookalikes

Orchardgrass is clumped and the stem base is flattened. Quackgrass has auricles that clasp the stem.

Parts to feed

Leaves and stems as hay or fresh cut.

New England timing

Best leaf is in May and June. It is cut for hay in early summer. Regrowth is modest in a dry August.

Harvest

Cut at early heading. Mature stems are low quality.

Preparation

Dry fully for hay, or feed fresh in a mix. Discard mold.
