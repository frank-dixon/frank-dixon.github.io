Forb Toxic to some animals Summer Fall

Solanum dulcamara · climbing nightshade

A climbing nightshade. It is not oriental bittersweet, which is a woody vine with red and yellow capsules.

Who can eat it

  • Rabbit Toxic

    Solanine and related glycoalkaloids. The berries and leaves poison livestock.

Identification

Purple flowers with yellow centers and bright red egg-shaped berries. Leaves are often lobed at the base.

Lookalikes

Oriental bittersweet is a woody twiner with orange capsules that split to show red seeds. That vine is not this plant, and it is not a feed either.

Parts to feed

Do not feed leaves or berries.

New England timing

It vines through New England hedges and flowers and fruits from midsummer into fall.

Harvest

Do not strip it off a fence for animals.

Preparation

Cooking is not a step in this guide. Leave the plant.
