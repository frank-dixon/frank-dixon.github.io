Grass Spring Summer

Phleum pratense · herd's grass

A standard cool-season hay grass of New England fields.

Who can eat it

  • Rabbit Safe

    A standard hay grass with no important toxin in normal stands.

    Use it as fiber or as one green in a mix. It does not replace a complete feed for birds or pigs.

Identification

A dense, cylindrical seed head like a small bottle brush. The stem base has a small swollen corm. The shoot is rolled.

Lookalikes

Orchardgrass grows in clumps and has a more open flower head. Reed canarygrass is taller and prefers wetter ground.

Parts to feed

Leaves and fine stems, fresh or as hay.

New England timing

Growth starts in May. The main hay cut in New England is often in June, with a second growth through August. Quality falls once the seed head is mature.

Harvest

Cut above the crown and leave stubble. Skip roadsides and any field sprayed with herbicide.

Preparation

Feed fresh in a mix, or dry it fully. Do not feed moldy hay.
