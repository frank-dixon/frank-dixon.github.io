Forb Toxic to some animals Summer Fall

Ageratina altissima

A woodland plant that poisons animals and can poison people who drink the milk of animals that ate it.

Who can eat it

  • Rabbit Toxic

    Tremetol in white snakeroot causes trembles in cattle and other livestock, and the toxin passes into milk.

    Do not feed it. Keep lactating animals off it.

Identification

Opposite leaves with three main veins and toothed edges, and flat clusters of white flower heads. It grows in shade.

Lookalikes

Boneset has leaves that the stem appears to grow through. Late white woodland flowers should not be fed on a guess.

Parts to feed

Do not feed any part.

New England timing

It flowers in late summer and fall in New England woods and edges. The toxin passes into milk, which is the historical milk-sickness story.

Harvest

Do not add it to browse from a woods edge.

Preparation

Drying does not make it safe. Do not use the milk of an animal that may have eaten it.
