Populus tremuloides

A poplar used as browse. Bigtooth aspen is similar. Bark in large amounts is the part to avoid.

Who can eat it

  • Rabbit Limit

    Aspen is ordinary browse. Phenolic glycosides in Populus bark are a reason to keep the offer to leaves and small twigs.

    Do not feed a large quantity of bark.

Identification

Rounded leaves that tremble, flattened leaf stalks, and smooth pale bark on young trees.

Lookalikes

Birch has a different bark and toothed leaves that do not tremble on flat stalks. Cherry smells like bitter almond when a twig is scratched.

Parts to feed

Leaves and small twigs. Not slabs of bark.

New England timing

Twigs and young leaves from late spring through summer. Winter twigs are browse when the leaves are gone. It is common in northern New England.

Harvest

Cut small branches. Do not strip a trunk.

Preparation

Feed fresh. A normal browse portion, not a diet of bark.
