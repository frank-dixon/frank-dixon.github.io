Forb Toxic to some animals Spring

Symplocarpus foetidus

A wetland arum. It smells skunky when torn and burns the mouth.

Identification

A mottled purple-brown hood in very early spring, then large oval leaves from the mud. Torn leaves smell skunky.

Lookalikes

False hellebore is taller, with pleated leaves up a stem, in wet meadows. Both are toxic.

Parts to feed

Do not feed leaves or roots.

New England timing

The spotted hood appears in early spring, sometimes through snow, in New England swamps. Huge leaves follow in May and June.

Harvest

Do not cut a swamp plant that smells like skunk.

Preparation

No preparation makes it feed.
