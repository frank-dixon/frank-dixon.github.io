Bramble Burrow

Private records for a small rabbitry: herd, pedigrees, litters and weights.

  • Django
  • Tailwind
  • PWA
  • Passkeys
Source

A record keeper for a small rabbitry. Each rabbit has a name or ear tag, sex, breed, color, birth date, photos and a status: breeding, growing out, sold or culled. Archiving keeps the record off the active list.

Sign in with a passkey or email and password, or look around as a guest. Photos are re-saved on upload, which strips EXIF data such as GPS location, and are only served to their owner. It installs as an app and keeps working offline.
