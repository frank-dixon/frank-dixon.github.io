Forage Guide

Which New England plants are safe, limited or toxic for small livestock.

  • Django
  • Tailwind
  • Passkeys
Open live

A reference for harvesting forage and feeding it to small livestock. Rabbits, chickens, ducks, geese, turkeys, quail, guinea pigs, goats, sheep, pigs and cattle each get a verdict (safe, limit or toxic) with a short reason. Covers Maine, New Hampshire, Vermont, Massachusetts, Rhode Island and Connecticut, wild or cultivated plants, including ordinary garden extras.

The whole guide reads with no account and there is no paywall. Favorites and notes stay in the browser, or on a free account (email and password, passkey optional).

A harvest reference, not a veterinary diagnosis.
