About

Front-end craft inside full-stack delivery

I’m a senior full-stack engineer with a front-end focus. Day to day that means owning the interfaces people actually use—clear component boundaries, thoughtful interaction design, and the quiet work of performance, accessibility, and usability that keeps a product feeling trustworthy.

At Undercover Tourist, I work across the stack in a travel and entertainment e-commerce setting (Disney, Universal, and related ticketing experiences). I care about architecture that ships: migrations that land cleanly, shared UI systems that teams can reuse, and Django-backed surfaces that stay maintainable as the product grows.

Earlier academic work in genomics and data (UMass Amherst) still feeds a side curiosity. That’s what led to my personal rabbit genetics experiments—small, exploratory projects where I connect domain interest to shipping software, not production products.
