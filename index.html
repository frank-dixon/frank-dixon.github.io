Node 01 · About

The web, a detour through the genome, then the web for good.

I started building for the web in 2004: small-business sites, then client-side apps for high-throughput print. Then I went back to school at UMass Amherst for a BSc in Plant, Soil & Insect Sciences and stayed on as a bioinformatics engineer (2008–2011), doing high-throughput genomic analysis and open-source tools for genetic markers.

In 2013 I came back to the web and stayed: Idea Agency, The Walker Group, Prudential, LATICRETE, and since 2021 Undercover Tourist. Most days I’m in the front end of a busy e-commerce site: Disney and Universal tickets, hotels, packages. Making sure checkout and content pages hold up when real people show up with real money. I’ve spent years moving us off an old front-end stack while the store stayed open.

The biology still leaks out, mostly as side projects about rabbits, hens and the plants around them.
