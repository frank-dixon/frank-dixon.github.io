01 — About

What I actually do

Story

Most days I am in the front end of a busy e-commerce site. Templates, JavaScript, CSS. Making sure checkout and content pages hold up when real people show up with real money.

At Undercover Tourist that means Disney and Universal packages, tickets, hotels. I have spent years moving us off an old front-end stack while the store stayed open. I also keep the shared UI pieces and Django admin tools from turning into a junk drawer.

Before this I did some genomics work at UMass Amherst. That still shows up as a rabbit genetics toy I built for myself. It is not a product. It is just something I wanted to click through.
