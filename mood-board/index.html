Do / don’t for the rebuild

One stab at direction. If this feels right, the landing redesign follows this board — not a second mood pass.

Do

  • Paper background full-bleed; ink text; turquoise for links/CTAs only
  • Big Fraunces headlines, generous margins, left-weighted copy
  • Projects as typographic rows / chapters
  • Shared quiet header across hub + demos
  • Motion only where it clarifies (optional, reduced-motion safe)

Don’t

  • Dark “AI SaaS” gradients, glassmorphism, neon glow
  • Card grids with drop shadows and rounded chrome
  • Hero Lottie / 3D / particle wallpaper
  • Too many fonts or accent colors
  • Case-study filler he won’t maintain
