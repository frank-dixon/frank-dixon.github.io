Easter Egger

Cross, not a standardized breed

Egg colorBlue, green or other (varies by hen)
Egg sizeMedium · Medium (Koops)
Egg shapeVaries by hen · not sourced
Eggs per year200–250
TemperamentNot sourced; Koops calls it very beginner-friendly
BroodinessSometimes goes broody (may sit on eggs to hatch them)
AutosexingNo
OriginNot sourced

Genetics

Not a breed: a cross of a regular brown layer with a blue layer, so egg color depends on which alleles each hen carries.

Breeding notes

Hens from one hatch can lay blue, green or, occasionally, tinted eggs.

Sources

  1. Koops Coops: Easter Eggers Accessed Oct 15, 2026. Supports: egg color, egg size, eggs per year, temperament, broodiness
    Blue egg; Medium; 200-250 per year; occasionally broody; very beginner-friendly
  2. Koops Coops: Olive Egger (Easter Egger definition) Accessed Oct 15, 2026. Supports: genetics
    Easter Eggers are produced by crossing a regular brown egg layer with a blue egg layer.
  3. PLOS Genetics: Wang Z. et al. (2013) An EAV-HP insertion in 5′ flanking region of SLCO1B3 causes blue eggshell in the chicken Accessed Oct 15, 2026. Supports: genetics
    Blue eggshell (oocyan) is caused by an EAV-HP insertion that up-regulates SLCO1B3 in the shell gland, increasing biliverdin uptake.
  4. PLOS ONE: Wragg D. et al. (2013) Endogenous retrovirus EAV-HP linked to blue egg phenotype in Mapuche fowl Accessed Oct 15, 2026. Supports: genetics
    Independent confirmation of the EAV-HP insertion in South American blue-egg fowl.

Egg shape: no source gives a typical shape for this breed, so it’s listed as “varies by hen.” Colors on screen are approximate.
