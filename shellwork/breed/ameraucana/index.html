Ameraucana

Egg sizeLarge · Large (McMurray)

OriginUnited States

Genetics

Blue shell from the oocyan (O) allele: an EAV-HP retroviral insertion upstream of SLCO1B3 makes the shell gland deposit biliverdin through the whole shell, so the inside is blue too. Dominant.

Breeding notes

One copy of the blue allele is enough; crossed onto a brown layer, daughters that inherit it lay green/olive.

Pairings

  • Ameraucana × Black Copper Marans → Olive green (reported by a breeder)
  • Ameraucana × Rhode Island Red → Blue/green mix (Easter Egger type) (reported by a breeder)

  3. PLOS Genetics: Wang Z. et al. (2013) An EAV-HP insertion in 5′ flanking region of SLCO1B3 causes blue eggshell in the chicken Accessed Oct 15, 2026. Supports: genetics
    Blue eggshell (oocyan) is caused by an EAV-HP insertion that up-regulates SLCO1B3 in the shell gland, increasing biliverdin uptake.
  4. PLOS ONE: Wragg D. et al. (2013) Endogenous retrovirus EAV-HP linked to blue egg phenotype in Mapuche fowl Accessed Oct 15, 2026. Supports: genetics
    Independent confirmation of the EAV-HP insertion in South American blue-egg fowl.
