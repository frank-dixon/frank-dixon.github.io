White Leghorn

Egg colorWhite
Egg sizeLarge · Large (McMurray, Koops); large to extra-large (Livestock Conservancy)
Egg shapeVaries by hen · not sourced
Eggs per year250–300
TemperamentVery active. McMurray: disposition 'poor'
BroodinessRarely goes broody (seldom sits on eggs to hatch them)
AutosexingUnknown
OriginNot sourced

How the shell gets its color

White shell: almost no pigment is deposited.

Breeding notes

Adds no brown pigment, so daughters of a Leghorn × blue-layer cross that carry the blue allele should lay blue (inferred from genetics).

Pairings

  • Araucana × White Leghorn → Blue (inferred from genetics)
