Penedesenca

Egg colorVery dark brown
Egg sizeExtra large · Extra large (Koops)
Egg shapeVaries by hen · not sourced
Eggs per year200–250
TemperamentNot sourced; Koops calls it good for beginners
BroodinessSometimes goes broody (may sit on eggs to hatch them)
AutosexingUnknown
OriginNot sourced

Genetics

Brown shell from protoporphyrin IX (see glossary).

Breeding notes

Figures come from one hatchery and haven't been confirmed elsewhere.

Sources

  1. Koops Coops: Crele Penedesenca Accessed Oct 15, 2026. Supports: egg color, egg size, eggs per year, temperament, broodiness
    Dark brown egg; XLarge; 200-250 per year; occasionally broody; good for beginners
  2. Poultry Science: Samiullah S., Roberts J.R., Chousalkar K. (2015) Eggshell color in brown-egg laying hens — a review Accessed Oct 15, 2026. Supports: genetics
    Brown color is protoporphyrin IX, concentrated in the outer shell and cuticle; color intensity tends to decline with hen age.

Egg shape: no source gives a typical shape for this breed, so it’s listed as “varies by hen.” Colors on screen are approximate.
