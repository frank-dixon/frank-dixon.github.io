Brown

Barred Plymouth Rock

Egg colorBrown
Egg sizeLarge · Sources differ: medium (McMurray), large (Conservancy), XL (Koops)
Egg shapeVaries by hen · not sourced
Eggs per year200–280
TemperamentCalm, docile
BroodinessSources differ: rarely goes broody (McMurray) or often broody (Koops)
AutosexingUnknown
OriginUnited States (exhibited in Boston, 1849)

Genetics

Brown shell from protoporphyrin IX (see glossary).

Sources

  1. Koops Coops: Barred Rock Accessed Oct 15, 2026. Supports: eggs per year, egg size, broodiness
    Brown egg; XLarge; 200-250 per year; broody
  2. McMurray Hatchery: Barred Rocks Accessed Oct 15, 2026. Supports: egg size, eggs per year, broodiness
    brown; medium; average 225 per year; 'about 220-280 eggs a year'; not very likely broody
  3. Poultry Science: Samiullah S., Roberts J.R., Chousalkar K. (2015) Eggshell color in brown-egg laying hens — a review Accessed Oct 15, 2026. Supports: genetics
    Brown color is protoporphyrin IX, concentrated in the outer shell and cuticle; color intensity tends to decline with hen age.
  4. The Livestock Conservancy: Plymouth Rock Chicken Accessed Oct 15, 2026. Supports: origin, egg color, egg size, temperament
    Developed in America in the mid-19th century, first exhibited in Boston in 1849; Brown; Large; Calm, Docile

Egg shape: no source gives a typical shape for this breed, so it’s listed as “varies by hen.” Colors on screen are approximate.
