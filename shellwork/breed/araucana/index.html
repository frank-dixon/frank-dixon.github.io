Genetics

Same oocyan/EAV-HP mechanism as other blue layers; the insertion was traced in South American (Mapuche) fowl.

Breeding notes

Rumpless and tufted traits are separate from egg color. Livestock Conservancy lists the breed as Critical.

Pairings

  • Araucana × White Leghorn → Blue (inferred from genetics)

Sources

  1. PLOS Genetics: Wang Z. et al. (2013) An EAV-HP insertion in 5′ flanking region of SLCO1B3 causes blue eggshell in the chicken Accessed Oct 15, 2026. Supports: genetics
    Blue eggshell (oocyan) is caused by an EAV-HP insertion that up-regulates SLCO1B3 in the shell gland, increasing biliverdin uptake.
  2. PLOS ONE: Wragg D. et al. (2013) Endogenous retrovirus EAV-HP linked to blue egg phenotype in Mapuche fowl Accessed Oct 15, 2026. Supports: genetics
    Independent confirmation of the EAV-HP insertion in South American blue-egg fowl.
  3. The Livestock Conservancy: Araucana Chicken Accessed Oct 15, 2026. Supports: egg color, egg size, temperament
    Status: Critical; Egg Color: Blue; Egg Size: Medium to Large; Temperament: Docile

Egg shape: no source gives a typical shape for this breed, so it’s listed as “varies by hen.” Colors on screen are approximate.
