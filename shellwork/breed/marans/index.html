How the shell gets its color

Dark color is protoporphyrin IX deposited late, mostly in the outer shell and cuticle, so it can partly rub off. Color usually fades over a laying season.

Breeding notes

Common brown parent for olive eggers.

Pairings

  • Ameraucana × Black Copper Marans → Olive green (reported by a breeder)
  • Cream Legbar × French Cuckoo Marans → Sage to olive green (reported by a breeder)
