Welsummer

Egg colorDark brown, often speckled
Egg sizeLarge · Large (McMurray)
Egg shapeVaries by hen · not sourced
Eggs per year220–280
TemperamentDocile
BroodinessSometimes goes broody (may sit on eggs to hatch them)
AutosexingNo
OriginNetherlands

Genetics

Brown pigment (protoporphyrin IX) applied in the shell gland; speckling is extra pigment laid on near the end.

Breeding notes

Koops Coops has crossed Welsummer with Cream Legbar to try for speckled green eggs.

Pairings

  • Cream Legbar × Welsummer → Green, possibly speckled (reported by a breeder)

Sources

  1. McMurray Hatchery: Welsummer Accessed Oct 15, 2026. Supports: origin, egg color, egg size, eggs per year, temperament, broodiness, autosexing
    Originated in Holland; docile; dark brown eggs; large; 220-280 eggs per year; somewhat likely broody; not auto sexing
  2. Poultry Science: Samiullah S., Roberts J.R., Chousalkar K. (2015) Eggshell color in brown-egg laying hens — a review Accessed Oct 15, 2026. Supports: genetics
    Brown color is protoporphyrin IX, concentrated in the outer shell and cuticle; color intensity tends to decline with hen age.

Egg shape: no source gives a typical shape for this breed, so it’s listed as “varies by hen.” Colors on screen are approximate.
