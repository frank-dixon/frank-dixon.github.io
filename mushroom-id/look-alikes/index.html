Chapter 1

Look-alikes

Pairs that people confuse. Learn the differences, then still have an expert check the mushroom itself.

Spread 01

Chanterelle or jack-o'-lantern

Chanterelle

Cantharellus species

Jack-o'-lantern

Omphalotus illudens · Poisonous

GrowsSingly or scattered, from soilTight clusters, on wood or buried roots
UnderneathBlunt, forking false ridgesSharp, true gills
FleshSolid white insideOrange throughout
VerdictLearn it with an expertPoisonous: severe vomiting and cramps

Spread 02

Morel or false morel

Morel

Morchella species

False morel

Gyromitra esculenta · Poisonous

CapHoneycomb of pits and ridgesBrain-like or wrinkled, not pitted
Cut lengthwiseOne hollow from cap tip to stem baseChambered or cottony inside
VerdictLearn it with an expertPoisonous: contains gyromitrin, which can kill

Deadly galerina

SizeDense clusters of medium capsSmall brown caps, 1 to 4 cm
Spore printWhiteRusty brown

Spread 04

Shaggy mane or common ink cap

Shaggy mane

Coprinus comatus

Common ink cap

Coprinopsis atramentaria · Poisonous

CapTall, white, shaggy upturned scalesGray-brown, smooth or faintly scaly
GrowthSingly or scatteredTight clusters
VerdictLearn it with an expertPoisonous with alcohol, even days later
For learning, not for eating. Never eat a wild mushroom based on this guide, a photo, or an app. Some deadly species look like common edible ones. Have a local expert check the actual mushroom in person first.If someone may have eaten a wild mushroom, call Poison Control right away: 1-800-222-1222 in the US.
