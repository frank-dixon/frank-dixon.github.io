All fungi · Bolete (sponge underneath)

Old man of the woods

Strobilomyces strobilaceus group

Where it grows

On soil under oaks and other hardwoods.

Season

Jul, Aug, Sep, Oct

Usual window. Local timing shifts with latitude, elevation, and rain.

Common in

Northeast, Southeast, Midwest and Great Lakes

How to know it

  • Cap covered in shaggy gray-black scales
  • Gray pores that bruise red then black
  • Shaggy stem

Dangerous look-alikes

No dangerous look-alikes; the black-and-white shaggy look is distinctive.

Your note

For learning, not for eating. Never eat a wild mushroom based on this guide, a photo, or an app. Some deadly species look like common edible ones.
